# DGIG R10 — numerical claims and inference limits

This page reports selected **manuscript values**, not new calculations from raw data in GitHub.

## Unchanged 15-minute / 1 K min^-1 rule

- Experimental units: six rule-controlled Weber heating runs, Tests 4–9, on one apparatus.
- Four alternative Level-4 thermocouples give 24 candidate/run events; all reach the unmodified criterion before the recorded commanded heater-off.
- Nearest alternative: TR404-085d (80 mm), source TR404-108e (95 mm).
- Its first-trigger lead relative to the recorded action: **23.59–31.12 min**, median **26.99 min**.
- Nearest-candidate heating Spearman association range: **0.99988–0.99999**. High association does not imply shutdown-event interchangeability.

## Exploratory corrections

- Temperature-trained affine and causal maps decrease temperature RMSE but do not improve nearest-channel source-event agreement.
- The high-capacity monotone fit produces four observed first events within a chosen five-minute, no-later-than-source window; the other two runs are right-censored. All four observed crossings subsequently recross upward on the recorded heater-on continuation.
- The one-sided timing window is **descriptive**, not an established physical tolerance.
- Configuration-blocked follow-up: train on homogeneous-inflow Tests 4, 6, 7, 8; evaluate D50 Tests 5 and 9 with a training-selected seven-knot monotone map.
- D50 Test 5: temperature RMSE **62.7714 to 3.1616 C** (**94.96% decrease**), but first event right-censored with source deadline missed.
- D50 Test 9: temperature RMSE **61.0447 to 2.4225 C** (**96.03% decrease**), but first event right-censored with source deadline missed.
- Both D50 pre-action search histories lie wholly within the model's training-input support. This experiment does not identify whether model capacity or configuration shift caused the missed events.

## Critical limits

The paper's measurement-event reconstruction is source-compatible, not the inaccessible original supervisory code. Candidate-triggered alternate heater shutdowns were **not physically executed**. These data do **not** establish energy savings, safety, population-wide sensor equivalence, model-wide failure, independent external controller validation or prospective closed-loop transfer.

The KIT VESPA analysis is mechanistic and neutral/mixed for the primary event, not an operational controller confirmation. Censoring remains censoring, not a successful late event. 

No new fitted results or accepted scientific claims are generated by the files currently committed to GitHub.
